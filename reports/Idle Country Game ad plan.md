# Fix the first minute, then buy players

Idle Country Game should **stop paid ads now** and keep them off until the first session is fixed. The ads aren't the problem. Every player they buy currently leaves within about a minute and a half and almost never comes back. The game's own dashboard puts it at the **0th percentile for average playtime (1.4 min against a genre median of 12.9 min) and for Day 1 retention (0.19% against 8.94%)**, with **0.00% Day 7 retention**, a 0.00% play-through rate, and 25 Robux a day in revenue, all while ads deliver about 1,530 plays a week ([Creator Hub overview](https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations/experiences/10769117926/overview)). Every source agrees that Roblox ads only amplify a game that already holds players. Some go further and say ad traffic on a weak game drags down the very metrics that organic discovery uses. So the plan runs in four phases. First, pause and set up (week 1). Second, a 3 to 4 week fix sprint, checked with cheap 10-credit "diagnostic bursts." Third, clear the "Ages 16+ and trusted friends" restriction (Roblox Kids and Select). Fourth, one disciplined launch campaign of about **12 credits a day for 7 to 10 days, roughly $85 to $120**, on Maximize Plays with 8 to 10 thumbnails, stopped the moment home-recommendation traffic overtakes ad traffic. Total paid spend through launch comes to about **$150 to $250 in ad credits**. The free levers (kashplaysYT videos, the group reward, update-tied codes, weekly updates) do the heavy lifting around it. The success numbers to judge by are a CTR of 2.5% or better, a cost per play near $0.008 to $0.02, average playtime over 10 minutes, and all six dashboard benchmarks above the 50th percentile while ads run.

## The current numbers make every ad dollar a loss

Start with payback, because it settles the question. Forum and YouTube developers consistently convert **1 ad credit to about 285 Robux, or roughly $1** ([DevForum, Jan 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/using-ads-manager-for-the-first-time/3342871); [Poke](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSVCfNHK2IE&t=416)). Roblox's own late-2025 benchmark for Maximize Plays was **$0.0073 per play**, and $0.0187 for genuinely new users ([DevForum, Nov 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-updates-home-search-combined-new-benchmarks-and-sunsetting-the-classic-flow/4084863)). Developers in 2026 report $0.01 to $0.024 ([DevForum, Jan 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-new-ad-manager-is-underperforming-and-i-am-considering-a-permanent-suspension-of-all-ad-spend/4269150)). That works out to **about 2 to 7 Robux of earnings needed per ad player just to break even**. Idle Country Game's payer conversion of 0.10% times its 17.4 R$ average spend per payer comes to roughly **0.02 Robux per player**. Those dashboard figures are daily averages, so true lifetime value is a bit higher, but with Day 7 retention at zero there is no "lifetime" to add. The game earns around one hundredth of what each bought player costs. At 2026 prices the 1,530 weekly ad plays cost somewhere around $12 to $35 a week, and the whole game makes about 175 Robux a week, which is less than one credit.

The damage goes beyond wasted money. Roblox says **"highly retentive games now get better ad distribution"** ([DevForum RDC recap, Oct 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-rdc-recap-earnings-roas-reporting-incrementality/4909790)). A 0th-percentile game therefore pays more per play than a healthy one. One developer's August 2026 data showed ad-sourced players playing about half as long as organic ones, with D1 at about 3% against 5 to 8%. **45% of ad players never pressed the first-screen button**, and the developer argues this pulls down the engagement signals behind organic ranking ([DevForum, Aug 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351)). The developer rasdev adds that Roblox judges games on a rolling 7-day window, so "you need a full week of better data to overwrite your old bad data." His summary of advertising an unready game is that it is "literally paying money to prove to Roblox that you don't have a game that is ready yet" ([rasdev](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7VhmLFkhwPU&t=196)).

To be fair to the other side: part of the bad numbers comes from the audience. Ad players are low-intent, and the 16+ restriction serves the game to a narrow, older pool. But 1.4 minutes of average play goes beyond what audience mix can explain. Something in the first minute isn't landing. A 0.00% play-through rate also says the tile itself isn't turning impressions into plays organically. The cautionary stories all look like this. Mulch spent 100,000 Robux on a buggy, lightly monetized game and earned 731 Robux back ([Mulch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B2vC4-QArWg&t=113)). Poke's game went from 115 CCU to zero the day ads stopped ([Poke](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VSVCfNHK2IE&t=582)). A 2024 campaign bought 200 CCU that fell to 0 to 4 within eight hours ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-experiences-moving-to-ads-manager/2661756?page=13)).

## Phase 1 and 2: pause, set up, and fix the first session (weeks 1 to 4)

### Week 1: stop the bleeding and get the accounts right

This week, pause or end the running campaign and switch off **auto-reload**. Auto-reload quietly buys a day's worth of credits whenever the balance runs out, and one developer lists leaving it on as a common way to overspend ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager); [LovJak](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=feVPsViqceo&t=256)). Unused credits come back when a campaign ends, and bot traffic is refunded about 16 days after the campaign ends ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager)). Let the transfer to CCountry Studios finish before building anything new. Ads Manager now supports **group ad accounts with a workspace selector** (Oct 2026), and group Robux can become group ad credits for anyone with the "Configure and spend group revenue" permission ([RDC recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-rdc-recap-earnings-roas-reporting-incrementality/4909790); [Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager)). The notes don't say how a group transfer affects Kids and Select status or existing campaigns, so check the game's audience-reach line and the campaign list after the transfer rather than assuming they carried over.

Next, make the ad cohort measurable. Ads Manager's **Advanced Join Options** let a campaign pass launch data that the game reads with `GetJoinData()` ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager)). Tag ad joins (for example `src=ad1`) and log a funnel: joined, pressed the first button, claimed a country, first upgrade, minute 3, minute 10, left. Ads Manager reports no retention by itself beyond Earnings and ROAS, so this tagging is how you'll know whether a fix worked for the players you paid for.

### Weeks 2 to 4: the fix sprint, with hard targets

The target is the genre median, because that is the level at which Roblox starts treating a game as worth recommending. Yann's rule of thumb: "If all of your stats are above the 50th percentile while on ads, you're in a good spot" ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw32DZtz4mQ&t=398)).

| Metric (7-day avg) | Now | Gate to unlock Phase 3 | Genre median | Genre 90th pct |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Average playtime | 1.4 min | **10 min or more** | 12.9 min | 30.3 min |
| Day 1 retention | 0.19% | **5% or more, aiming at 8.9%** | 8.94% | 14.68% |
| Day 7 retention | 0.00% | **0.5% or more** | 0.78% | 2.47% |
| Payer conversion | 0.10% | **0.4% or more** | 0.46% | 2.17% |
| Avg revenue per payer | 17.4 R$ | **50 R$ or more** | 62.9 R$ | 252.1 R$ |
| Play-through rate | 0.00% | **2% or more** | 2.14% | 4.41% |

Medians from the [Creator Hub overview](https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations/experiences/10769117926/overview). rasdev independently names 10+ minute sessions as the engagement bar ([rasdev](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7VhmLFkhwPU&t=42)).

The fixes with the strongest evidence come first. **Give the first screen one obvious action**, such as a big "Claim your country" button on the world map, so the 45% do-nothing bounce seen in other games has nowhere to happen ([DevForum, Aug 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351)). Make sure the first 60 seconds deliver exactly what the thumbnails promise: your colour spreading across the map, army numbers climbing, a first raid. Keep the game light on low-end phones. Roblox is about **80% mobile** ([Newzoo via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-roblox-as-a-platform-in-2025)), and frame-rate trouble on low-end devices was one of the reasons Yann's simulator underperformed ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bw2ty2T46z4)). He also warns that bad D7 is "a core loop issue" that daily rewards won't fix ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw32DZtz4mQ&t=286)). So if D7 stays at zero after onboarding is fixed, the next things to look at are the reasons to come back: offline income collection, raid revenge, and alliance obligations.

Monetization has to be ready before ad day one, or the paid traffic produces no useful revenue data. In Yann's dragon simulator the top earners were a **"10x spins" dev product, an exclusive pet and rebirth skips**, ahead of 2x-cash passes ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bw2ty2T46z4)). He also halves pass and product prices during week one of ads to get a realistic read on conversion ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw32DZtz4mQ&t=286)). For Idle Country Game that suggests a cheap starter pack, crate multi-buys priced so bigger packs are better value, and a time-boost product. The Earnings objective is off the table for now regardless. It requires **ARPU of 6 Robux or more** ([RDC recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-rdc-recap-earnings-roas-reporting-incrementality/4909790)), and the game is far below that.

### Diagnostic bursts: tiny paid tests that measure, not grow

With one concurrent player, the game has too little organic traffic to tell whether a fix worked. So after each major fix, run a **10-credit diagnostic burst: 5 credits a day for 2 days**, which matches the historic campaign floor ([DevForum, May 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-ads-manager-upgrades/3659656)). At 2026 prices that buys very roughly 400 to 1,000 plays, which is enough to read average playtime, first-button bounce and the funnel. Day 1 retention from a sample that size is noisy. Budget **three bursts across the sprint (about 30 credits, around $30)**. Read results only after 48 hours, because plays and earnings show up late ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager)). Each burst doubles as a thumbnail test, covered below. Move to Phase 3 only when a burst shows average playtime of 10 minutes or more and the dashboard's 7-day numbers are trending toward the gate.

## Phase 3: clear the 16+ wall before chasing scale

The "Ages 16+ and trusted friends" label shrinks the paid audience. Staff confirmed that **"games ineligible for under-16 users won't show ads to that demographic automatically"** ([DevForum, May 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-updates-maximize-earnings-attribution-updates-new-tile-in-beta-other-updates/4623762)). Any Plays campaign before eligibility buys from a smaller, pricier 16+ pool, and age-targeting below 16 does nothing. Under-15s are Roblox's biggest segment ([Newzoo via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-roblox-as-a-platform-in-2025)), so clearing this wall is the biggest single cost reduction available.

Getting cleared takes two things. On the account side, the owner needs **ID verification, 2-step verification, good standing, and a refundable 1,000 Robux publishing fee** per game (or two consecutive months of Premium/Plus). On the game side, it needs a **threshold of highly engaged players** ([Kids and Select launch post](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-global-launch-upcoming-updates-to-eligibility-ads-manager-and-expedited-review/4685717)). The two official sources disagree on the number. The June 2026 announcement says 500 highly engaged players, with maintenance lowered to 25 over 60 days. The current Creator Docs say **250 unique plays from highly engaged players within 60 days**, where "highly engaged" means account age, play history, and a Roblox purchase in the last 60 days ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select)). The docs are newer, so treat 250 as the likely target and confirm it on the dashboard. Pay the 1,000 R$ fee and finish ID/2SV during the fix sprint so paperwork isn't what holds things up.

You can reach the threshold three ways. The cheapest is organic: point kashplaysYT viewers aged 16+ and their friends at the game once onboarding works. Keep in mind that under-16 viewers can't join until the gate clears (except as Trusted Friends). The second is the **Engagement objective (beta)**, which Roblox built for exactly this situation. It targets players whose sessions count toward the threshold. Expect a **higher cost per play than Plays**, allow "several days to produce meaningful results," and **pause it yourself**, because it doesn't stop when you qualify ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager); [Kids and Select launch post](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-global-launch-upcoming-updates-to-eligibility-ads-manager-and-expedited-review/4685717)). Recommended settings: daily budget of **10 credits for up to 7 days (70 credits cap)**, with the same 8 to 10 thumbnails as the main campaign. Check eligibility daily and pause the same day it clears. The third route is **Expedited Review: 100,000 Robux, up to 48 hours, skips the threshold**, refundable after 90 days in good standing ([Kids and Select launch post](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-global-launch-upcoming-updates-to-eligibility-ads-manager-and-expedited-review/4685717)). Yann said in hindsight he "would have paid" it, because his game only got home recommendations after qualifying around day 13 ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bw2ty2T46z4&t=156)). For a game earning 25 Robux a day this is effectively a 100,000 Robux, three-month loan that only comes back if the game stays eligible. Use it only if those Robux are sitting idle elsewhere and the gate metrics are already met. Never use it to skip the fix sprint.

## Phase 4: one launch campaign, settings and day-by-day schedule

### Exact Ads Manager settings

Once the game is Kids/Select eligible and the gate metrics hold, create one campaign from the CCountry Studios ad account with these settings:

| Setting | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | **Plays** (Maximize Plays) | Default for discovery. Earnings needs ARPU 6+ R$, and Engagement is only for the gate ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager)) |
| Audience | **All Players** | Roblox's own team says new games should leave audiences open. Recent (10K+) and Lapsed (20K+) aren't available yet ([Roblox Learn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5HcH-9E7USc&t=42)) |
| Advanced targeting | **Off**: all regions, all devices, all ages, all genders | "I've actually gotten the best results using the default settings" ([bubDEV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7z8rfg_jBuk&t=363)). Narrowing raises cost ([Roblox Learn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5HcH-9E7USc&t=42)) |
| Budget type | **Daily**, never lifetime | Budget type can't be changed after publishing ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager)). Steady daily flow is the creator consensus ([CallMeDogey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7QfaQe4rsq8&t=386)) |
| Daily budget | **12 credits (about $12)** | Within the $10 to $20 range that 2026 creators use after Kids/Select ([MoseyTube77](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9UxSCiz3us&t=60); [Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw32DZtz4mQ&t=242)) |
| Duration | **10 days scheduled, expected to stop between day 7 and day 14** | Long enough for the algorithm to evaluate the game, and creators say ads should "never last more than 2 weeks" ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw32DZtz4mQ&t=286)) |
| Creatives | **8 to 10 thumbnails** (16:9, 1920x1080) | Impressions are spread across them, so the campaign doubles as a built-in A/B test ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager)) |
| Start | **The day a content update goes live** | New players land on fresh content, and the update-themed creatives match it |
| Payment | Robux converted to credits if available, otherwise card (18+ only, $5 first charge) | Conversion can't be undone. Check the conversion screen, since Oct 2026 added "enhanced valuation" for US 18+ Robux ([RDC recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-rdc-recap-earnings-roas-reporting-incrementality/4909790)) |
| Auto-reload | **Off** | Prevents surprise top-ups |

There's a minority view on two of these. bubDEV uses a **lifetime** budget of about 250 credits over 5 days ([bubDEV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7z8rfg_jBuk&t=160)), and older advice favors targeting tier-1 countries for richer players ([med_v3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7ZKZo0enAMU&t=111)). Yann did find that a low-spending country mix hurt his revenue ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bw2ty2T46z4&t=111)), but nobody has published a controlled geo test under the current system. Start broad. Try a tier-1 geo test only in a later campaign if the first one shows healthy retention but weak spend.

### The schedule and the decision on each day

**Day 1: don't touch anything.** Campaigns sit in "Learning" for the first 24 hours ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager)). Clicks show up before plays. Raul saw 3,000 clicks and 17 plays on day one, and the plays appeared the next day ([Raul](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vYv8DA7pqU&t=344)). Spend the day watching the error log, the funnel and live servers, and fix bugs. **Days 2 to 4: prune creatives.** Remove or replace any thumbnail under about 1.5% CTR and keep the winners running ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw32DZtz4mQ&t=330); [MoseyTube77](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9UxSCiz3us&t=209)). The docs now list creatives as editable after publishing, though a mid-2026 creator still had to cancel and restart to swap them ([Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager); [Raul](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vYv8DA7pqU&t=217)). Ship a fix update the same day the stats show a problem ([Tizzy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMnhg3fn_M&t=864)). **Day 5: be honest.** If playtime or D1 is far off the gate, stop and go back to fixing. If home-recommendation impressions are starting to appear, continue ([MoseyTube77](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9UxSCiz3us&t=161)). **Days 7 to 14: taper and stop.** Seibo cut from 14 to 10 to 3 credits a day as home-recommendation plays overtook sponsored plays, and his game reached about 900 CCU ([Seibo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FmAD02FS_3k&t=24)). Tizzy's rule is the cleanest: **stop once weekly home-recommendation impressions reach or pass weekly ad impressions**. He also notes that first home impressions usually take at least a week ([Tizzy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMnhg3fn_M&t=864)). Live budget changes are allowed, so taper with the budget field rather than restarting ([DevForum, May 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-updates-maximize-earnings-attribution-updates-new-tile-in-beta-other-updates/4623762)).

## Benchmarks, creatives, and the free levers that multiply spend

### The scorecard

| Metric | Stop or fix | Average | Good, keep going |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTR (clicks / impressions) | under 1.5% | about 2% | **2.5%+**, great at 3.5%+ |
| Cost per play | over $0.025 | $0.01 to $0.02 | **$0.008 or less** |
| Plays per impression | under 2% | 3.5% | 4%+ |
| Average playtime (ad cohort) | under 5 min | 10 min | 12.9 min+ (genre median) |
| Day 1 retention while on ads | under 3% | 5% | 8.9%+ |
| Payback (earnings / credits x 285 R$) | under 0.3 | 0.3 to 1.0 | 1.0+ |
| Home-rec impressions vs ad impressions | still near zero by day 7 | rising | **equal or higher: stop ads** |

The CTR and cost-per-play bars come from MoseyTube77 and Yann, who both target "2.5% click-through rate or better, and around $0.008 cost per play or better" ([MoseyTube77](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9UxSCiz3us&t=161); [Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw32DZtz4mQ&t=352)), plus Raul's "1% is bad and getting 2% is actually just average" ([Raul](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vYv8DA7pqU&t=273)). The 2026 cost-per-play bands and plays-per-impression come from DevForum reports ([DevForum, Oct 2025](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-are-sponsored-ads-suddenly-x3-more-expensive/3983531)). The payback cut-offs are a forum rule of thumb, and it's unconfirmed whether Ads Manager's "Earnings" figure is gross or net of Roblox's cut. Be honest about payback: few small idle games make their money back from the ad players directly. Yann's well-run dragon simulator earned **60K Robux back on a 100K spend** in 28 days ([Yann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bw2ty2T46z4&t=22)), and only about two-thirds of the larger games in the Earnings beta passed 100% return ([RDC recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-rdc-recap-earnings-roas-reporting-incrementality/4909790)). The real payoff, when it comes, is home-recommendation traffic, which is why that row decides when you stop.

The sources do conflict on whether ads can trigger recommendations at all. Roblox says **"ad performance should not impact your organic rank in Recommended for You"** ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-tests-sponsored-experiences-to-appear-within-recommended-for-you-on-home/3938575)). Creators report that ads were what got their games into recommendations. CallMeDogey says about $50 of ads "only gains the game about 10,000, maybe 20,000 visits and then it picked up on the algorithm" ([CallMeDogey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7QfaQe4rsq8&t=113)), and MoseyTube77's $179 campaign led to a 3,000-CCU game ([MoseyTube77](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5zz5ao0zV_Y&t=112)). Both can be true. Ads don't push rank up directly, but they hand the recommender a sample of players. If those players stay and spend, Roblox keeps showing the game. If they bounce, as they do today, the ads only confirm the game isn't ready. That is why the fix sprint comes first.

### How many creatives, and what goes on them

Launch with **8 to 10 thumbnails across four concepts**, because Hypershot raised CTR by scaling "from 10 to 25 unique creatives" ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise)) and Tizzy advises "at least 10" ([Tizzy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMnhg3fn_M&t=726)). The four concepts follow the genre's proven hooks: the map turning your colour, army numbers climbing, raiding a rival's capital, and a boss fight. The existing five comic-style thumbnails (Rule the World, Take Their Capital, Conquer the World map, Raid, Defeat the Warlord) already cover all four, so make one or two extra variants of each, changing a single element such as the colour scheme, the crop, or the text. Roblox's rules: **16:9 at 1920x1080 and under 3 MB**, nothing important near the bottom edge where the player count sits, and no promotional or claim text such as "FREE" or "#1". Short gameplay text like "TAKE THEIR CAPITAL" is fine ([Thumbnail docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails); [Promotional thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/promotional-thumbnails)). Check every one at phone size. Mobile tiles render around 110 pixels wide, where only one dominant shape, strong contrast and warm reds and oranges still read ([RoWatcher News](https://rowatcher.com/news/why-your-roblox-thumbnail-works-on-desktop-and-fails-on-mobile)). A 2023 test found static art beat gameplay-trailer thumbnails, which got under 1% CTR ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-experiments-with-sponsored-and-user-ads/2420807)). Treat the new video tiles rolling out in October 2026 as a later experiment, not the default. Expect CTR to fade. CallMeDogey saw a drop of 0.8 points or more in under a week ([CallMeDogey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SjjPGduApZU&t=23)), so plan new variants each week. When a winner emerges, make it the game's main thumbnail too. That attacks the 0.00% organic play-through rate directly. The title deserves the same scrutiny. Tizzy's CTR went from 1.54% to 3.27% after renaming a game to a simple verb-plus-noun "core fantasy" ([Tizzy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMnhg3fn_M)), and genre leaders add rotating update tags such as "[UPD]" or "[🎭Puppets]" to their titles ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/4983034985)).

### Free levers, in order of value

**kashplaysYT** is the cheapest player source available. It reaches people who already play Roblox and costs nothing per play. Use it twice: during the fix sprint for high-intent testers (and highly engaged players toward the gate), and on launch day so the first ad cohort lands in servers that already have real players. Since under-16 viewers are blocked until the game clears Kids/Select, save the big push video for after eligibility. **The group reward** should copy Idle Mafia Game, which grants a **permanent +10% income for joining the group and liking the game** ([AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/roblox-idle-mafia-game-codes-and-early-progression-guide/)). The game already has a +10% join gift, so make it permanent, prompt it in the first few minutes, and repoint it to CCountry Studios after the transfer. **Update-tied codes** earn free coverage from code sites, which is how Mini War and Build An Ant Empire get steady articles ([iGeeksBlog](https://www.igeeksblog.com/roblox-mini-war-codes/); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/build-an-ant-empire-codes/)). Release one or two with every update and announce them in the video and on the group wall. **Update cadence** comes next: every genre leader checked had been updated within days, and Roblox advises testing new thumbnails with each major update ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/conquer-the-world-ww2-4983034985); [Thumbnail docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)). Aim for a **weekly update with a title tag**, and schedule each paid campaign to start on an update day. The existing invite-a-friend quest fits the alliance theme. Tie its reward to alliance bonuses so inviting feels like recruiting. Later, once the game has 20,000+ lapsed players, the Lapsed Players audience becomes a cheap way to win people back after each big update ([Roblox Learn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5HcH-9E7USc&t=42)).

## Conclusion

For a game in this state, the money question is really a sequencing question. Ad spend is a test fee for a recommendation system that grades how players behave, not a way to buy growth. Right now the game fails that test in 90 seconds, so every credit spent pays Roblox to file the game as "not ready" and makes later ads more expensive through retention-weighted distribution. The 16+ restriction adds a second wall that also rewards fixing first, since the players who count toward Kids/Select are exactly the engaged ones a working first session keeps. That makes the right budget small and staged: about $30 of diagnostic bursts that only measure, an optional capped push through the gate, and one 7 to 14 day launch with stop rules decided in advance.

Where the evidence is thin, say so plainly. No source published cost-per-play or CTR numbers specific to strategy or idle-conquest games. The highly-engaged-player threshold (250 vs 500) and the Robux-to-credit rate need checking on screen, and nobody has tested geo-targeting under the current Ads Manager. Treat this plan's numbers as starting bets and replace them with the game's own campaign data after the first burst. If one number deserves daily attention, it is average playtime for the ad cohort. Once it clears 10 minutes, almost everything else in the plan follows.
