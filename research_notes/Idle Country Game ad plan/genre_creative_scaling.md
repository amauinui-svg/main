# Genre-specific growth and ad creative for an idle country-conquest strategy game on Roblox

Research date: 2026-10-06. Stats-site numbers are snapshots taken on that date unless noted. Third-party stats sites (RoWatcher, Bloxodes) and blog posts (rowatcher.com/news, obby.fun) are secondary sources and are flagged as such.

## Case studies of comparable Roblox games (launch, ads, update cadence, growth)

### Takeaway
The country/war-map genre has proven ceilings on Roblox: Conquer The World WW2 holds ~17K CCU and ~292M visits as the #2 Strategy game, and Mini War hit a 126K all-time peak in June 2026. Newcomer Pixel Conquest peaked at 11K CCU about two months after its August 2026 launch. None of these games publishes ad spend, so the link between ads and growth for them cannot be shown. What they visibly share is very frequent updates (all showed "last updated" minutes to days ago) and update-tied codes and group rewards.

### Cited Findings
- **Conquer The World WW2** (creator Marian_Dev): created Aug 13, 2023; 17.4K CCU, 21.4K 24h peak, 291.7M visits, 870.5K favorites, 81% rating, All Ages; global rank #63, **#2 in Strategy genre**, best global rank #49 (reached Sept 29, 2026); last updated about 4 days before the snapshot. — [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/conquer-the-world-ww2-4983034985). The title carries a rotating emoji/update tag (e.g. "[🎭Puppets]", "[🚀]"), which shows it re-labels its title for each update. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/4983034985)
- **Mini War** (M&M Community): 9K CCU now, **all-time peak 126K CCU (June 2026)**, 351.4M visits, 1.2M favorites, 96.5% like ratio (794K likes). RoWatcher lists "release March 2026" and the Simulation genre. The game says it is still in beta and was updated minutes before the snapshot. Its title also rotates tags ("[X3]", "[UPD]", "[💥]", "5M"). — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/9837612476/mini-war). Note: the place ID (9837612476) suggests the place was created years earlier, so "March 2026" is likely a relaunch or public release date. This is unverified.
- Mini War gets heavy code-site coverage (press framing: "Strengthen Your Country's Military Power to Dominate Nations"), with codes published monthly through June–July 2026. — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/roblox-mini-war-codes-june-2026-strengthen-your-countrys-military-power-dominate-nations-163435); [iGeeksBlog](https://www.igeeksblog.com/roblox-mini-war-codes/)
- **Pixel Conquest** (8Yoonie): released Aug 2026; all-time peak 11K CCU (Oct 2026), now 5K CCU, 36.2M visits, 24K favorites, 81.2% like ratio; updated minutes before the snapshot. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10764297006/pixel-conquest)
- **Idle Mafia Game** (group "Stacks of Cash"): idle-income properties earn offline, active jobs give XP, three meters (Stamina/Energy/Health) gate actions, PvP raiding steals unbanked cash, bank with a 10% deposit fee, Family (co-op) system, and a property ladder from a $500 Newsstand to a $400K warehouse. Joining the group plus liking the game gives a **permanent +10% property income**. Codes are tied to launch and updates (RELEASE, MAFIA, UPDATE, IDLEMAFIAGAME). — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/roblox-idle-mafia-game-codes-and-early-progression-guide/)
- **Build An Ant Empire** (Insect Kingdom studio): roll ants, gather Food, stack Cash, grow the colony. Codes give 15-minute cash boosts and "new codes typically release alongside game updates and new ant additions." — [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/build-an-ant-empire-codes/)
- Roblox's own ads case study (Hypershot): "By scaling from 10 to 25 unique creatives, the Hypershot team identified and validated high-performing visual concepts that optimized their clickthrough rate." It ran campaigns against All Players and Recent Players. Roblox's aggregate claims for games that run ads: "150% lift in impressions," "24% increase in plays," "16% increase in playtime." — [Roblox Creator Docs: Advertising](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise)
- Counter-evidence on paid traffic quality (DevForum, Aug 29, 2026, game "GET OUT", an extraction shooter): with Sponsored Ads on, average playtime was about 11–12 min against 28–30 min with ads off. Session time was 9–10 min against 19–21 min. D1 retention was about 3% with ads against 5–8% without. **45% of ad-sourced players never pressed the first-screen button**, and the spend added only 5–10 CCU. The developer argues this drags down the engagement metrics that drive organic ranking. Roblox staff said it was "assigned to our team for review" (Aug 31, 2026). Another dev (Sept 13) agreed that "paid traffic is usually worse than organic traffic." — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351)

### Inferences
- The genre leaders grew mostly on retention plus update momentum (high favorites-to-visits ratios, constant update tags). Ads amplify a game that already retains; they don't replace that.
- Idle Mafia Game is the closest structural template (idle income + raids + co-op group + update codes + a group/like reward). Its income-boost reward is a cheap, proven pattern to copy.
- Given the GET OUT data, Idle Country Game's first screen must have one obvious action, such as a "Claim your country" button on the world map. That protects the ad-driven cohort from the 45% do-nothing bounce.

### Gaps
- No public ad-spend, CTR or CPP numbers found for Idle Mafia Game, Conquer The World WW2, Mini War or Pixel Conquest. Idle Mafia Game's homepage-ad activity could not be verified from public sources; RoWatcher/Bloxodes stats for Idle Mafia Game weren't retrieved.
- No stats retrieved for Control Europe, Rise of Nations, 2 Player Raid Tycoon, or Build An Ant Empire's CCU/visits (Build An Ant Empire may be on [RoTrends](https://rotrends.com/game/8435447325), not fetched).
- Update cadence was inferred from "last updated" timestamps and title tags, not from changelogs.

## Who plays this genre on Roblox and what hooks convert them

### Takeaway
Roblox is mobile-first (about 80% of users access it on mobile) and skews young, but 13+ is the faster-growing segment. Ads Manager lets you target by location, age, gender, genre and device, so a country-conquest game can target the Strategy and Simulation genre audiences directly. No genre-specific demographic split for strategy was found.

### Cited Findings
- 80% of Roblox users access via mobile, and 24% play only on mobile. Under-15 is the largest segment. In Q2 2025 the 13+ cohort grew 54% and under-13 grew 22%. The top genres by daily visits are Roleplay & Avatar Sim (116M), Simulation (110M), Platformer (70M), Survival (70M) and Action (59M); strategy and tycoon weren't broken out. About 1.25% of DAU purchase daily. — [Newzoo via GameDevReports, 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-roblox-as-a-platform-in-2025)
- Ads Manager targeting: All, New (never played or 180+ days inactive), Recent (needs 10K+ recent players), Lapsed (needs 20K+), plus advanced filters by **location, age, gender, genre, and device type**. — [Roblox Creator Docs: Ads Manager](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager)
- Roblox reached 151M+ DAU as of Q3 2025. — [Outlook Respawn, Jan 2026](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/amp/story/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-unveils-homepage-ad-format-and-expands-buying-at-ces-2026)
- Hook evidence from the genre's framing: Mini War code coverage leads with "Strengthen Your Country's Military Power to Dominate Nations" ([Player.One](https://www.player.one/roblox-mini-war-codes-june-2026-strengthen-your-countrys-military-power-dominate-nations-163435)). Idle Mafia Game's loop centres on an income ladder plus PvP raids that steal unbanked cash ([AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/roblox-idle-mafia-game-codes-and-early-progression-guide/)).
- Mini War is categorised as Simulation even though it is a war/country game, so the genre audience overlaps across Strategy and Simulation. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/9837612476/mini-war)

### Inferences
- Likely converting hooks for this audience: (1) the map turning your colour (territory growth shown visually), (2) army size and numbers going up (idle progression), (3) rivalry and raids (taking another player's land), (4) co-op alliances. These match what the genre leaders put in their titles and press framing.
- Target both Strategy and Simulation genre audiences, mobile-first, and test 13+ separately from all ages, since 13+ is growing faster.

### Gaps
- No public age, region or device breakdown specific to the strategy/country genre on Roblox. Regional skew (e.g., whether WW2/country games over-index in particular countries) is undocumented.

## Thumbnail, icon and ad-creative best practices

### Takeaway
Roblox's official rules: 16:9 at 1920×1080 for thumbnails and ads, square and at least 512×512 for icons; preview icons at about 150×150; keep key elements away from the bottom edge; no promotional or claim text; imagery must honestly show the game. Run 2–5 thumbnails for personalization and refresh them with major updates. Ads campaigns can rotate up to 10 creatives, and Hypershot's CTR rose when it scaled to 25 creatives. Practitioner sources (lower confidence) push one dominant subject, warm high-contrast colours, a close-cropped character face, and little or no text at mobile size.

### Cited Findings
- Thumbnails: 16:9, ideally 1920×1080, under 3 MB. Show "unique and relevant imagery on what players should expect when they join." "Avoid placing any essential text or elements at the bottom of the thumbnail, as it may potentially be covered by metadata like the player count." Activate **2–5 thumbnails** for the personalization algorithm and keep several active. "Test new thumbnails with major game updates, then maintain consistency until the next update." Video thumbnails must be authentic in-game footage. — [Roblox Creator Docs: Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)
- Text rule: "Overlay text sparingly and only to describe gameplay contexts such as 'Collect coins to boost jumps.' Do not include any text, visuals, or audio that is an advertisement, promotion, or subjective claim, for example '50% off' or 'Free UGC!'." — [Roblox Creator Docs: Promotional thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/promotional-thumbnails)
- Icons: square, at least 512×512. Preview at about 150×150 so details stay clear. Use imagery that reflects the actual experience; bright saturated colours for whimsical games, strong contrast for intense genres. The official icon doc gives no guidance on text or faces. — [Roblox Creator Docs: Experience icons](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-icons)
- Ads: sponsored thumbnails on Home and in Search, 16:9, **up to 10 thumbnails per campaign** evenly distributed. Bidding is automated **cost-per-play**. Objectives are Plays, Earnings (limited) and Engagement. Funding is by card or Robux converted to ad credits (13+ only; irreversible), and unused credits are refunded. — [Roblox Creator Docs: Ads Manager](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager)
- Hypershot raised CTR by scaling from 10 to 25 unique creatives. — [Roblox Creator Docs: Advertising](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise)
- Practitioner (blog, July 21, 2026): mobile discovery renders tiles at about **110×110 px**, desktop at about 260×146. What survives is silhouette clarity (one dominant shape), strong contrast, and warm colours (orange/yellow/red) against Roblox's grey UI. Text becomes illegible at 110×110. Test by viewing at 110×110 for 3 seconds, doing a grayscale check, and scroll-testing beside real games on a phone. Design mobile-first. — [RoWatcher News](https://rowatcher.com/news/why-your-roblox-thumbnail-works-on-desktop-and-fails-on-mobile)
- Practitioner (blog, Sept 9, 2026): close-cropped characters beat wide scenic shots. Pick one emotion (danger, power, surprise, humor). Text should be at least 40px in the original, with five or fewer visual elements. The post claims "CTR lifts of 20–40%" for a single high-contrast subject and that faces outperform scenery. Recommended test: a two-week A/B on one variable. These claims are unsourced, so treat them as directional. — [RoWatcher News](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-thumbnail-is-a-conversion-ad-stop-designing-it-like-art)
- First-minute consistency: 45% of ad-sourced players never pressed the first-screen button in one game's data. — [DevForum, Aug 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351). Roblox's analytics track first-play bounce in windows under 60s and 61–180s. — [obby.fun, Apr 22, 2026](https://www.obby.fun/blog/how-to-make-roblox-game-popular)
- Seasonal and update creatives: the genre leaders rotate update tags and emojis in their titles (Conquer The World WW2 "[🎭Puppets]"; Mini War "[X3]", "[UPD]", "5M"). — [RoWatcher CTW](https://rowatcher.com/games/4983034985); [RoWatcher Mini War](https://rowatcher.com/games/9837612476/x3-mini-war)

### Inferences
- For Idle Country Game: one dominant subject (a Roblox avatar general or a big army), a world map visibly split between your colour and the enemy's, warm reds and oranges for conquest, and at most 2–4 large words describing gameplay ("TAKE THE WORLD", "RAID NATIONS"). Never use offer or claim text ("FREE", "#1", "50% OFF").
- The ad thumbnail should show the exact first screen (the world map with a "claim country" action). The first 60 seconds must deliver the promise the ad showed, or ad players bounce and drag down organic ranking.
- Start with 5–10 ad creatives across 3–4 concepts (map conquest, army growth, raid or rivalry, boss), cut the losers after a test window, and replace them with new variants. Hypershot's experience suggests going up to about 25.
- Swap in update-themed creatives and title tags with each update (e.g., "[⚔️ RAIDS]", "[👑 LAWS]").

### Gaps
- No official Roblox benchmark CTR or CPP numbers for ads were found. No controlled data on faces versus no faces or on text word counts from Roblox itself; the numbers here come from unsourced practitioner blogs.

## Non-paid growth levers that multiply ad spend, and their cost efficiency versus ads

### Takeaway
The genre's leaders lean on update cadence (frequent updates plus title tags), update-tied codes, and group/like rewards (Idle Mafia Game: permanent +10% income). Roblox recommends refreshing thumbnails with major updates. A developer report shows paid traffic retains worse than organic, so these levers should come before and alongside ads. No hard cost comparison between creator sponsorships and Roblox ads was found.

### Cited Findings
- Group plus like reward: Idle Mafia Game gives a permanent +10% property income for joining the group and liking. Codes are named for release and updates. — [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/roblox-idle-mafia-game-codes-and-early-progression-guide/)
- Update-tied codes: Build An Ant Empire releases codes with updates and new ants ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/build-an-ant-empire-codes/)). Mini War codes were refreshed monthly and widely covered by code sites ([iGeeksBlog](https://www.igeeksblog.com/roblox-mini-war-codes/), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/mini-war-codes-3384680/)). Code-site articles are free external discovery.
- Update cadence: all genre leaders checked showed "last updated" minutes to days ago ([Bloxodes CTW](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/conquer-the-world-ww2-4983034985), [RoWatcher Mini War](https://rowatcher.com/games/9837612476/mini-war), [RoWatcher Pixel Conquest](https://rowatcher.com/games/10764297006/pixel-conquest)). Roblox advises testing new thumbnails at each major update ([Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)).
- Lapsed-player ads exist as a re-engagement tool once a game has 20K+ lapsed players, and Recent Players targeting needs 10K+. — [Ads Manager docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager)
- Paid versus organic quality: ad cohorts showed roughly half the session time and lower D1 retention than organic in one 2026 report. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351)
- Retention measurement: Creator Analytics gives D1, D2–7 and D8–28 retention and first-play bounce windows, with benchmark scorecards for games with 100+ DAU. "There is no universal retention or session-time target that fits every genre." — [obby.fun, Apr 2026](https://www.obby.fun/blog/how-to-make-roblox-game-popular)
- Creator marketplaces list hundreds of Roblox content creators for hire (e.g., 455 verified on CollabPals) but publish no standard rates. — [CollabPals](https://www.collabpals.com/hire/gaming/roblox-content)
- Roblox will introduce CPM-based fees for creator brand deals starting in 2027 (this applies to brand deals on-platform). — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-sets-cpm-based-fees-for-creator-brand-deals-starting-in-2027/)

### Inferences
- Cheapest multipliers, roughly in order of cost efficiency: (1) Kash's own kashplaysYT channel. Organic videos and Shorts of the game cost no ad spend, reach an audience that already plays Roblox, and act as a pre-launch spike that seeds the first ad cohort with high-intent players. (2) A group/like reward copying Idle Mafia Game's permanent +10% income. (3) Update-tied codes, which earn free code-site coverage. (4) Weekly update tags in the title plus refreshed thumbnails. (5) A Discord or group wall for alliance coordination, which fits the alliances feature. (6) Invite-a-friend rewards that grant alliance bonuses. Then Roblox ads, aimed at a game that already retains.
- Time ad bursts to update days, so new players land on fresh content and update-themed creatives.
- Watch D1 and first-60s bounce for the ad cohort separately (Creator Analytics). If the ad cohort's D1 falls well below organic (as in the GET OUT case), pause and fix onboarding before scaling.

### Gaps
- No published data on YouTuber/TikTok sponsorship prices for Roblox game promotion, or on cost per player acquired through creators versus Roblox ads. The rolearn.dev creator-growth article was JS-rendered and unreadable.
- No data on the effect of experience notifications or invite prompts for this genre specifically.
- No public cost-per-play benchmarks for Roblox Sponsored Ads in 2025–2026.
