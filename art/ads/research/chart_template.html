The player we are selling to

Thinking as the player scrolling the Roblox homepage looking for a country game. Each thumbnail gets under a second. It has to answer one question: can I control a country, take land and rule the world with an army?

"When looking for a country game I'm looking for something where I can control my country, take over land and rule the world with the army."

Flow recipe

  1. Project: Google Flow, Nano Banana 2, 16:9, x2 outputs.
  2. Attach two house style refs (sref_01 to sref_16, uploaded from art/ads/style_refs). Pick the two closest in composition: map scenes use sref_16, armies use sref_14, rulers use sref_01, sref_03 or sref_06, alliances use sref_02.
  3. Never attach a competitor thumbnail. Their concept goes into the scene text, their art style stays out.
  4. Prompt = scene text (title first, then the scene) + the style block below, word for word.
  5. Always write the exact title in quotes. Without it Flow invents its own title.
  6. Review every output: real game features only, at most two named avatars (bacon hair, classic noob), no real flags, no tobacco, alcohol or real insignia, no garbled text, no extra limbs.
